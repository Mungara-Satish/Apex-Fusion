## SLIDE 2: Problem Statement & Market Opportunity
- **Title**: Challenges in 10th-Grade Board Preparation
- **Key Challenges**:
  1. **Curriculum Fragmentation**: Generic learning apps treat CBSE, ICSE, and State Boards identically, ignoring board-specific nuances (e.g., ICSE GST & Matrices vs. State Board Metallurgy).
  2. **Doubt Resolution Bottlenecks**: Students get stuck late at night with no immediate step-by-step mathematical proof or marking scheme guidance.
  3. **Parental Blind Spots**: Parents lack actionable visibility into their child's conceptual weaknesses and attendance.
  4. **Inefficient Faculty Workflows**: Tutors struggle with fragmented tools for live classes, local computer uploads, Google Drive streams, and timetable management.
- **Market Opportunity**: Over 25+ Million students appear for 10th board exams in India annually, seeking structured revision and personalized mentoring.
- **Speaker Notes**:
  > "Class 10 is the foundational academic milestone for millions of students. Yet existing platforms fail to deliver board-specific precision. Apex Fusion was architected to bridge these exact gaps."
